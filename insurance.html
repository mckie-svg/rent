Exclusions

Please note that insurance coverage may be voided if the vehicle is used in violation of our Terms of Service. Key exclusions include:

  • Off-road driving on non-paved surfaces.
  • Driving under the influence of drugs or alcohol.
  • Using the vehicle for commercial purposes or racing.
  • Allowing unverified drivers to operate the vehicle.

How to make a claim?

In the event of an accident or damage, your safety is our priority.

  1. Ensure everyone is safe and contact local authorities if necessary.
  2. Document the damage with clear photos from multiple angles.
  3. Notify the Host immediately through our in-app messaging.
  4. Contact our 24/7 Claims Support team at +1 (800) RYDE-SAFE.
  5. File an official report through the "My Bookings" section of your dashboard.
